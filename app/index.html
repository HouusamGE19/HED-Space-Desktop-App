HED Space is a private desktop media experience built around the collection you already own. Movies, series, anime, childhood favourites, music, and immersive atmospheres come together in one cinematic interface — with intelligent library scanning, season and episode organisation, custom artwork, resume progress, high-quality local playback, and a presentation designed to feel closer to a premium streaming service than a file browser.

Your media stays where you put it. HED Space indexes it locally, preserves your folder structure, and gives you control over how every title looks and plays.
